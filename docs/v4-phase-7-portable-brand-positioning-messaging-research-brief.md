# V4 Phase 7 — Portable brand, positioning, messaging, proof, and narrative research brief

**Status:** next approved Growth evidence gate  
**Decision to settle:** what portable core should be shared across product, service, and website work; what remains distinct in Brand Strategy, Expert Positioning, Copywriting, Copy Editing, Marketing Psychology, and Storytelling; and what must remain specialist spatial material.

## Use only the prompt below

Start a fresh source-backed Deep Research task. Paste only the text inside the code block; do not attach this document or ask the research tool to review earlier reports. It must conduct this study itself and return the first report.

```text
You are the research agent. Conduct this source-backed study yourself now.

Do not ask for prior reports, capability inventories, project files, or attachments. Do not write a campaign, make a public claim, contact people, publish material, collect personal data, or take any external action. Produce a research report that a separate reviewer will later use to revise an AI Product Studio's skills and references.

Decision to support
We need the smallest durable operating model for portable brand, positioning, messaging, proof, and narrative across apps, websites, professional services, product concepts, and client-facing offers. The Studio sometimes builds high-end spatial/cinematic websites, but it must also work for ordinary products and services. We need to decide what is genuinely portable, what should remain a specialist spatial reference, what should be a conditional resource rather than a skill, and what should not become an OS instruction at all.

The aim is not to turn branding into a fixed workshop, force every task through a messaging framework, or reduce storytelling to generic persuasion. It is to help an advanced model make better decisions about meaning, audience, differentiation, proof, narrative, and clarity while remaining truthful and context-sensitive.

Current assets to assess without assuming their current classification or size is correct
1. `brand-strategy`: currently a deep Spatial capability for interior/spatial brands, including perception gap, taste world, creative authority, proof, and premium signal.
2. `expert-positioning`: currently a professional-services capability influenced by one positioning book, including diagnosis, selectivity, commercial posture, and qualification.
3. `storytelling`: currently a deep Spatial/cinematic website capability including atmosphere, taste, transformation, proof, method, and inquiry.
4. `copywriting`: currently drafts persuasive website/product/service language.
5. `copy-editing`: currently improves existing language while preserving intended meaning.
6. `marketing-psychology`: currently contains behavioural-science and decision-friction material, with a risk of overgeneralised persuasion rules.

Operating context and constraints
- The Studio is one accountable operator building digital products, websites, professional services, and occasionally spatial experiences. It works from Nigeria and may serve international clients. Country, language, industry, culture, buyer, channel, and legal context are conditional project facts, not universal instructions.
- Product Thinking owns product problem, value trade-offs, scope, and build decisions. Research Analysis owns general research method. UI/UX owns interface design, accessibility, and task success. Page CRO owns page/flow friction diagnosis. Prospect Research and Sales Enablement own specialised opportunity and buyer-material preparation.
- A shared Customer, Market, and Demand Evidence reference already handles decision-to-evidence matching. Do not duplicate it; state where positioning, messaging, or narrative must rely on its evidence.
- AI may draft, compare, analyse, and surface contradictions. It must not invent customer language, testimonials, credentials, outcomes, category proof, social proof, cultural insight, or demand. It cannot infer a private audience motive from a stereotype or make external claims without approval.
- Every recommended method must be proportionate. A small copy edit or clear page rewrite must remain direct work. Bigger evidence and decision records are justified only when uncertainty or consequence makes them useful.

Research questions
1. Define and distinguish: brand strategy, brand identity/visual execution, positioning, segmentation, value proposition, category framing, message architecture, narrative/storytelling, copywriting, copy editing, proof/claims, tone of voice, behavioural insight, conversion communication, and actual product value. Which distinctions are essential for an AI operating system, and which are merely different labels for the same job?
2. What portable decisions recur across products, services, and websites: audience/context, problem, alternatives, category, differentiated promise, reason to believe, proof, constraints, non-fit, message hierarchy, narrative sequence, and next action? Which are only conditionally relevant?
3. What evidence can support each decision, and what cannot? Specifically distinguish customer language, observed behaviour, public alternatives, client statements, brand history, case studies, qualitative interviews, usability/comprehension observations, and AI-generated hypotheses. State the limits on any claim that brand positioning, narrative, psychological principle, or copy formula causes conversion or commercial success.
4. Assess the six current assets individually. For each, decide whether to preserve, update, keep but reclassify, split into portable core plus conditional specialist references, share a reference with another asset, merge only at a routing level, demote to a reference, defer, or retire. Protect specialist spatial value unless a moved section has a named destination and loading condition.
5. Evaluate whether Brand Strategy and Storytelling should have portable roles beyond spatial work. If yes, define their smallest portable jobs and specify exactly what remains specialist: atmosphere, spatial sequence, materiality, cultural symbolism, visual taste, cinematic choreography, luxury residential codes, or other relevant content. Do not assume portable means shallow.
6. Evaluate whether Expert Positioning should remain distinct from Brand Strategy. Preserve useful professional-service judgement, but test whether any rule derived from a single book is overly absolute, sector-specific, or hostile to legitimate early-stage/low-budget work.
7. Evaluate whether Copywriting, Copy Editing, and Storytelling should remain separately activated modes even if they share evidence/proof rules. Explain the user-job and failure-mode differences rather than merging because all involve words.
8. Evaluate the proper role of Marketing Psychology. Separate ethical behavioural insight, cognitive load, framing, trust, decision friction, and accessible communication from manipulation, fake scarcity, personality stereotyping, vulnerable-audience exploitation, dark patterns, and universal psychological-trigger claims.
9. Identify what, if anything, materially changed in 2024–2026 for AI-assisted content, search/distribution, personal branding, professional services, credibility, synthetic media, and buyer trust. Separate durable changes from provider/channel-specific trends and hype. Treat Nigeria-specific or platform-specific material as conditional reference material.
10. Compare three implementation shapes for the portable core: (A) six separate skills with shared references; (B) a single unified Growth skill; (C) a small portable foundation plus distinct Brand, Positioning, Narrative, Draft, and Edit modes. Evaluate decision quality, discoverability, operational burden, overlap, reversibility, and preservation of specialist depth for a one-person Studio.
11. Specify clean activation/exclusion/handoff boundaries with Product Thinking, Customer/Market/Demand Evidence, Research Analysis, UI/UX, Page CRO, Prospect Research, Sales Enablement, and spatial work.
12. Propose only the next 2–3 targeted studies or local audits that are truly necessary after this report. Do not manufacture a research programme.

Source requirements
- Prefer original academic research, systematic reviews/meta-analyses, official standards/regulator guidance, primary platform documentation, well-described usability research, and primary industry datasets with methods and dates.
- Books, branding agencies, sales methodologies, consultancies, platform marketing, practitioner essays, and influencer content may be labelled secondary context only. They must not be the sole basis of a universal rule.
- Cite every material factual claim with a direct working link and publication or verification date.
- Separate sourced fact, inference, recommendation, and open uncertainty throughout.
- State country, culture, sector, buyer role, channel, sample, and methodological limits that could change any recommendation.
- Do not provide legal advice. Flag conditions where jurisdiction, data use, consent, advertising, or consumer-protection review is required.

Required output
1. Executive synthesis: the smallest useful portable core and what it is not.
2. A decision/boundary map distinguishing all named concepts and adjacent Studio capabilities.
3. Evidence-to-claim table for portable brand, positioning, message, narrative, proof, draft, edit, and behavioural-insight decisions.
4. Six-asset decision ledger with preservation proof for any proposed move, merge, split, demotion, or retirement.
5. Portable-versus-spatial matrix for Brand Strategy and Storytelling.
6. Comparison of the three implementation shapes, with recommendation, tradeoffs, and conditions that would reverse it.
7. AI-age changes: durable, conditional, and hype/noise to reject.
8. Ethical and authority boundary table, including public claims and external effects.
9. Candidate rules table: rule, source/evidence type, scope/limit, proposed destination, and preserve/adapt/defer/reject decision.
10. Positive, negative-routing, and authority-stop scenarios for any future assets.
11. The next 2–3 evidence gates only.
12. Disconfirmation: what would make the recommended structure wrong.

Return the complete report now. Do not ask for any additional attachment.
```

## Review gate after return

Score the returned report against source quality, claim discipline, context fit, evidence limits, ethical/authority fit, operational usefulness, and reversibility. No skill may be deleted, merged, or materially rewritten until every adopted rule has a source/limitation/destination record and a preservation path for specialist material.
