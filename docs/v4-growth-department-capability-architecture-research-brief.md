# Focused research prompt — Growth department capability architecture

Copy the prompt below into a deep-research tool as a single message. It is a **research assignment**. Conduct the research and return the deliverable described here; do not ask for a returned report, workspace access, or additional brief before beginning.

---

## Assignment

Design the smallest complete **Growth, Market, and Commercial Intelligence department** for a one-person, AI-assisted Product Studio.

The Studio can build digital products, websites, SaaS applications, professional services, client offers, and occasional high-end spatial/cinematic websites. It needs to understand what is worth building, who it serves, how to make a credible offer, how to communicate value honestly, how to find appropriate opportunities, how to improve a live experience, and how to learn from real evidence over time.

Start from a blank sheet. Do **not** assume the current skill catalogue is complete, well grouped, or even the right starting point. First define the department's real jobs and boundaries. Only then compare the target against the existing assets.

This is not a request for generic “growth hacking,” a funnel diagram, a list of marketing channels, a social-media playbook, or one skill for every business topic. It must decide which capabilities contain reusable judgement that an AI Product Studio genuinely needs, which should be references or recipes, which belong to other departments, and which should wait for an actual recurring need.

## Operating context

One human is accountable for decisions. AI agents may research, analyse, draft, critique, and prepare proposals, but may not autonomously contact people, publish, spend money, collect sensitive data, change prices, run advertising, or represent claims as fact without explicit approval.

The Studio may operate across Nigeria and international clients. Country, channel, sector, privacy, consumer-protection, platform, and payment issues are contextual constraints, not universal rules. The department must work with little or no traffic, imperfect data, and incomplete customer access; it must not imitate a large venture-backed growth team.

## Existing inventory to compare only after the blank-sheet map

The current Growth pack has seven active skills:

1. `copy-editing` — improve existing copy without inventing facts.
2. `copywriting` — draft evidence-bounded product, service, and website copy.
3. `expert-positioning` — professional-services posture, inquiry paths, and commercial framing.
4. `marketing-psychology` — currently restricted to ethical, evidence-limited behavioural hypotheses.
5. `page-cro` — diagnose conversion friction on a page or flow.
6. `prospect-research` — research and score prospects for a current Cinematic Digital Showroom offer; it must stay useful for that work.
7. `sales-enablement` — prepare collateral, pitch materials, objection handling, and demos; it does not send or commit externally.

Related but separate assets include:

- `product-thinking`, `research-analysis`, `competitor-profiling`, `ui-ux`, `testing`, and `reference-intelligence` in the General pack;
- `brand-strategy` and `storytelling`, which currently contain protected spatial/cinematic expertise and must not be flattened;
- a conditional Customer/Market/Demand Evidence reference, and a conditional Meaning and Evidence Foundation reference.

The current pack has one optional `marketing-copy` coordinator. Do not assume it deserves to remain a workflow: distinguish a real stateful workflow from a skill, recipe, reference, agent-owned procedure, or direct task.

## Decisions the study must make

1. What is the department's mission, its inputs, outputs, decision rights, and explicit exclusions?
2. What are the durable capability jobs needed to serve products, services, website projects, and client work from early discovery through credible demand and learning?
3. Which jobs belong to Product Strategy, Design, Engineering, Quality, or a specialist pack rather than Growth?
4. Which jobs need a standalone skill, which should extend an existing skill, which are a selectively loaded reference/recipe, and which must be deferred until real work proves the need?
5. Does the Studio need a portable brand-strategy capability beyond the protected spatial `brand-strategy` skill? If yes, specify whether to expand that skill with profile-gated references or introduce a distinct portable capability.
6. Does the Studio need a durable capability for offer architecture, packaging, pricing, scoping, or value communication across products and professional services? State the boundary between research, commercial judgement, legal/financial advice, and user approval.
7. Does it need a measurement/learning capability now, or should measurement remain a conditional mode under Product Thinking, Page CRO, Testing, and evidence references until a live product supplies real data?
8. What is the right relationship between prospect research, sales enablement, outreach, relationship management, distribution, SEO/content, partnerships, and customer retention? Do not create a standalone capability merely because the category exists.
9. Which activities need a genuine workflow, and which should be selected by the future Growth/Commercial agent as internal procedures? Identify any current workflow that should be demoted or merged.
10. What evidence, provenance, uncertainty labels, privacy boundaries, and approval stops are mandatory for this department without turning simple copy or small reversible work into ceremony?

## Evaluation standard for a proposed skill

Approve a new standalone skill only if it has all of the following:

- a recurring Studio decision or deliverable with a distinct failure mode;
- reusable judgement that current skills and conditional references cannot supply cleanly;
- a narrow activation boundary and neighbouring exclusions;
- a clear owner and handoff to other departments;
- evidence that it improves decision quality without overriding model reasoning or forcing a ritual;
- a safe, proportionate verification path.

For every rejected or deferred category, explain what absorbs the work instead and what observable future trigger would reopen the decision.

## Research standards

- Prefer primary sources, systematic reviews, high-quality empirical studies, recognised standards, and official regulator guidance where a factual claim is made.
- Distinguish evidence from an inference, practitioner convention, and your Studio recommendation.
- Treat findings as context-limited by market, product type, traffic, country, channel, and sample. Do not turn a case study, a bias label, or a framework into a universal law.
- Use current official sources for volatile platform, policy, analytics, and channel claims; include direct URLs and dates.
- Do not give legal, tax, investment, or compliance advice. Identify situations needing qualified local review.
- Reject fabricated proof, fake urgency, deceptive comparisons, dark patterns, exploitative targeting, mass spam, unsupported performance claims, or AI-generated evidence presented as customer fact.

## Required deliverable

Return a concise but substantive decision report with these sections:

1. **Executive verdict:** the department’s purpose, its minimum complete shape, and the major approach rejected.
2. **Blank-sheet capability map:** for each capability job, show decision served, inputs, outputs, owner, activation, exclusions, dependency/handoff, and failure mode.
3. **Current-versus-target gap ledger:** map every existing asset above to Keep / Expand / Split / Move / Merge / Demote / Retire. Then list each genuinely missing candidate with the same decision and evidence.
4. **Standalone-skill test:** for each candidate, decide Skill / Existing-skill extension / Reference or recipe / Agent procedure / Workflow / Defer. Give the reason and the disconfirmation trigger.
5. **Agent collaboration model:** describe how a future Growth/Commercial agent collaborates with Product Strategy, Design, Engineering, and Quality without becoming a permanent autonomous sales or marketing swarm.
6. **Workflow decision:** name only the jobs that truly need stateful coordination and approval gates. Identify current entries that should become direct capabilities or internal procedures.
7. **Four scenarios:** a Nigerian service/website client, a general SaaS product, a new digital product with no traction, and an existing product with limited real data. Show which capabilities activate and which do not.
8. **Evidence and authority protocol:** claim types, evidence levels, uncertainty labels, data/privacy constraints, approval gates, and what remains read-only.
9. **Implementation ledger:** no more than 15 concrete changes, ordered by dependency. Mark each as immediate, evidence-gated, real-project-gated, or reject.
10. **Disconfirmation:** what future evidence or Studio use would prove this proposed department structure wrong.

The final answer must make it possible to decide whether the existing seven skills are sufficient. “No new skills” is acceptable only if the gap ledger explicitly demonstrates that every department job has a clear, non-duplicative owner, activation rule, and safe verification path.
