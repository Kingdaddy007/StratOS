# V4 Phase 7 — Growth primary-source baseline

**Status:** supporting evidence only; checked 2026-08-19  
**Purpose:** give the Growth research assessment a small set of durable, primary or official anchors. This does not replace the two focused research briefs, create universal legal advice, or justify a substantive skill-method rewrite by itself.

## Candidate durable rules

These rules are candidates for the later research synthesis, not automatic additions to every skill:

1. **Truthful claims and meaningful choice:** do not present deception, hidden material information, fabricated proof, false urgency, fake endorsement, or privacy manipulation as persuasion expertise.
2. **Accessibility is a boundary, not a conversion trick:** Growth may identify a form or decision-flow concern, but UI/accessibility work needs the appropriate Design/UX route and project-specific verification.
3. **Experiments are evidence with limits:** formulate a decision-changing hypothesis, verify that the measurement/control still represents the claim, and state population/time/generalisation limits. Do not require an A/B test when it cannot credibly change the decision.
4. **Research is not outreach authority:** account research, scoring, and drafting remain distinct from sending messages, publishing, CRM updates, advertising purchases, or other external effects.
5. **Legal/channel claims remain conditional:** direct marketing, privacy, consent, platform, and advertising requirements need current jurisdiction- and channel-specific verification. The OS may surface the boundary but must not provide blanket legal conclusions.

## What remains open
