## What is accepted

- **One foundation, five distinct jobs.** Brand, Positioning, Narrative, Draft, and Edit are different decisions and fail in different ways. Drafting and editing will not be merged; no generic “Growth super-skill” will replace them.
- **Meaning before wording; evidence before assertion.** The shared foundation records only the decision objects needed for the work, classifies claim status, and leaves factual gaps visible.
- **Spatial depth is protected.** Existing `brand-strategy` and `storytelling` are spatial skills today. Their whole contents are not promoted to the General profile. Portable elements can be abstracted later only after a cross-domain routing audit demonstrates they work without losing the spatial judgement.
- **Behavioural insight is conditional.** It informs clarity, cognitive load, trust, accessibility, and testable framing hypotheses. It is not a catalogue of conversion triggers or a universal persuasion engine.
- **Direct work remains direct.** A known, low-consequence rewrite does not create a research, narrative, or positioning ceremony.

## Corrections and limits

1. “Always available” means **shipped and discoverable**, not always loaded into every task. The implementation uses a manifest reference and router link, not a baseline or forced prompt.
2. `prospect-research` and `sales-enablement` are outside the report’s six-asset ledger. Their separate safety and external-effect boundaries stay intact.
3. “Brand mode” and “Narrative mode” are a target capability shape, not a permission to change current profile registration. The immediate safe step is a shared reference; later skill changes require task-routing fixtures.
4. The NIST discussion has a citation-number typo in the report (it cites `[8]` where the NIST entry is `[15]`). The underlying statement was rechecked against NIST’s 2024 Generative AI Profile; no rule is adopted solely from the typo.
5. The FTC health-products citation is too narrow to be treated as a general global advertising rule. The OS retains the narrower operational rule: do not invent or overstate claims; flag jurisdiction-specific review when a material public claim needs it.
6. C2PA provenance is useful where relevant but optional and not a truth certificate. It will not create a mandatory media-recording workflow.
7. Any human reviewer/recruitment step in the proposed evidence gates is an external research activity; it needs explicit approval before it begins. Local routing fixtures can be run without that action.

## Implemented foundation

- [Meaning and Evidence Foundation](../global/reference/meaning-and-evidence-foundation.md) is a conditional shared reference, registered in the General payload so every supported host can discover it.
- The Studio router now names it only when a material brand, positioning, narrative, message, proof, or claim decision is uncertain.
- It does not activate the Growth or Spatial pack, does not replace the owning capability, and never authorises an external effect.

## Preserved boundaries

- `brand-strategy` retains spatial identity, material, taste-world, and premium-signal judgement.
- `storytelling` retains spatial narrative, proof choreography, and cinematic/experience architecture.
- `expert-positioning` remains the professional-services route, pending a separate review of its absolute book-derived rules.
- `copywriting` and `copy-editing` remain distinct, pending a separate update of their legacy context paths and evidence/claim guards.
- `marketing-psychology` remains available but needs a dedicated revision before it can be treated as an ethical conditional reference rather than a broad mental-model catalogue.
- `page-cro`, `prospect-research`, and `sales-enablement` remain outside this implementation batch.
