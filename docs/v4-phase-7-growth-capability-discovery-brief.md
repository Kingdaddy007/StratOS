# V4 Phase 7 — Growth capability discovery brief

**Status:** ready to run before any further Growth skill rewrite  
**Purpose:** decide the smallest useful Growth capability map for a personal AI Product Studio; it does **not** presume that the current seven skills are complete, correctly classified, or each worth preserving as a standalone skill.

## Why this comes first

The current Growth pack is an inherited inventory, not a conclusion. It contains:

- `copy-editing`
- `copywriting`
- `expert-positioning`
- `marketing-psychology`
- `page-cro`
- `prospect-research`
- `sales-enablement`

Two related capabilities are currently classified as Spatial-only: `brand-strategy` and `storytelling`. That may be correct for their *current specialist versions*, but it does not answer whether an evidence-based, product-and-service version belongs in Growth, should be shared across packs, or should remain specialist.

The decision cannot be made by counting skills, following fashionable marketing advice, or treating a recent AI trend as a durable rule. First establish the jobs the Studio must perform and their clean boundaries. Only then decide whether a job needs a short skill, a conditional reference, a workflow, a project-specific playbook, or nothing new at all.

## How to use this brief

Run this one self-contained prompt in a source-backed deep-research tool. Do not attach other Anti-Gravity documents. If either later Growth study has already been run, keep it: its evidence may be used in the next synthesis, but it must not be treated as proof that the current seven-skill taxonomy is correct.

```text
Conduct a rigorous, source-backed capability-discovery study for the Growth function of a personal AI Product Studio.

Decision to support
We are not asking you to write marketing copy, launch a campaign, or design an enormous marketing department. We need to decide the smallest, durable set of capabilities that would help one accountable operator build digital products and websites, position them honestly, validate demand, improve a client or product's communication, prepare sales material, and pursue appropriate opportunities. The result will guide whether existing AI-assistant skills should be kept, upgraded, merged, moved to conditional references, separated into specialist packs, or not created at all.

Operating context
- The Studio builds apps, websites, client-facing services, and product concepts. General engineering, product thinking, design, testing, architecture, security, and research already exist outside the optional Growth pack.
- The operator sometimes serves spatial/design clients, but the Growth capability map must also work for ordinary digital products and professional services. It must not assume every task is a luxury interior-design offer.
- The operator works in Nigeria and may serve international clients. Treat country, industry, language, audience, channel, and regulation as project context unless durable evidence proves otherwise.
- AI can accelerate research and drafting, but it must not invent market facts, personalisation, proof, user research, consent, demand, or confidence. A framework, a funnel metric, a heatmap, or an AI-generated persona is not proof by itself.
- No capability may authorise outreach, publishing, CRM updates, ad spend, purchases, data scraping, or other external effects. It may research, analyse, recommend, and prepare drafts for human approval.

Current inventory to assess, not to preserve automatically
1. copy-editing
2. copywriting
3. expert-positioning (adapted from a professional-services positioning book)
4. marketing-psychology
5. page-cro
6. prospect-research (currently a local client-finding dossier tool)
7. sales-enablement
8. brand-strategy (currently spatial-only)
9. storytelling (currently spatial-only)

Research questions
1. What distinct jobs should a lean personal product studio be able to perform across market/customer understanding, segmentation or ideal-customer fit, brand and positioning, messaging and narrative, copy and editing, demand/acquisition, conversion/activation/retention, sales/discovery/proposal support, and measurement/learning? Do not assume every named area deserves a standalone skill.
2. Which of those jobs belong in: (a) the General core, (b) an optional Growth pack, (c) a Spatial or other specialist pack, (d) a project-specific playbook/reference, or (e) nowhere in an AI operating system? Explain the boundaries with Product Thinking, UI/UX, Storytelling, Research Analysis, and Design rather than duplicating them.
3. Assess the nine current assets. For each, recommend: keep and update; keep but reclassify; merge; split into entrypoint plus conditional references; turn into a recipe/tool adapter; defer; or retire. Name the evidence and uncertainty behind the recommendation.
4. Specifically assess whether brand strategy and storytelling have a portable Growth role beyond their current spatial use. If so, specify the minimum portable job and what must remain specialist. If not, explain why not.
5. What changes, if any, in 2024–2026 materially alter durable practice for AI-assisted product marketing, branding, professional services, research, copy, conversion, and sales preparation? Separate real, evidence-supported changes from platform hype and trend claims. Treat any Nigeria-specific finding as conditional context, not a universal OS rule.
6. What common marketing, personal-branding, social-media, growth-hacking, outreach, behavioural-science, or AI-automation advice should explicitly not become a universal instruction? Address manipulation, unsupported claims, privacy/consent, dark patterns, fake scarcity, discriminatory targeting, and low-confidence inference.
7. Recommend no more than 6–8 top-level Growth capabilities and no more than 10 total active Growth assets unless stronger evidence proves more are necessary. For every recommendation, say why a strong model cannot reliably handle the job with General capability alone, and what a focused skill would add without boxing its reasoning in.
8. Identify the smallest next research sequence. State which existing or proposed capability genuinely needs a dedicated, source-backed deep study before any rewrite, which can be modernised through a local audit, and which should wait until a real project exposes a gap.

Source and evidence requirements
- Prefer original research, official standards/regulator guidance, original datasets, well-described empirical studies, reputable usability research, and clearly dated primary platform documentation.
- Use books, agencies, marketing platforms, practitioner essays, and influencer content only as labelled secondary context. Never make them the sole support for a universal rule.
- Cite every material factual claim with a direct working link and publication or verification date.
- Clearly mark every statement as sourced fact, inference, recommendation, or open uncertainty. Flag material limits caused by country, sector, audience, channel, or sample size.
- Do not claim legal compliance. Identify where jurisdiction, channel, relationship, platform terms, or qualified counsel changes the answer.

Required output
1. An executive answer: what the Growth function is and is not for this Studio.
2. A capability map with job, activation, exclusions, owner/boundary, correct asset form, current-asset mapping, evidence strength, and recommendation.
3. A decision table for the nine current assets: preserve, update, move, merge, split, demote, defer, or retire—with reasoning.
4. A small “AI-age changes” table: durable changes, conditional changes, and hype/noise to reject.
5. A safety and authority table for research, drafting, validation, and external effects.
6. A sequenced research plan that is deliberately narrow: the next 2–4 studies only, including the decision each will settle.
7. A short disconfirmation section: what evidence would make this proposed map wrong.

Do not produce a generic marketing playbook, campaign ideas, job advertisements, or an unbounded list of skills. The quality bar is a clear, evidence-conscious operating map that leaves the AI free to reason while making recurring high-stakes decisions more reliable.
```

## Decision gate after the report returns

1. Score the report for source quality, boundary clarity, applicability to a one-person product studio, distinction between fact and recommendation, and usefulness for asset decisions.
2. Compare it against the current inventory and the two prepared focused briefs.
3. Approve the smallest evidence-supported capability map.
4. Only then run the 2–4 targeted studies the map identifies. The existing positioning/CRO and prospect/sales briefs are candidates, not mandatory truth.
5. Rewrite one capability at a time, preserving useful depth and adding a behaviour test for every material route or authority claim.
