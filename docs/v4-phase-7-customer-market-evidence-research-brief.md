## Use only the prompt below

Start a fresh source-backed Deep Research task. Paste only the text inside the code block; do not attach this document or ask the research tool to review earlier reports. It must conduct this study itself and return the first report.

```text
You are the research agent. Conduct this source-backed study yourself now.

Do not ask for prior reports, capability inventories, project files, or attachments. Do not write marketing copy, contact people, send surveys, scrape data, or perform external actions. Your task is to produce a research report that a separate reviewer will later use to decide how an AI Product Studio should handle customer, market, and demand evidence.

Decision to support
We need to decide whether a personal AI Product Studio needs a dedicated optional capability for Customer, Market, and Demand Evidence—or whether that job should instead be a shared mode/reference under existing Product Thinking and Research Analysis. The Studio builds apps, websites, client-facing services, and product concepts. It needs credible evidence to decide what to build, whom to serve, how to position an offer, whether an offer is worth pursuing, and whether a message is understood.

This study must prevent two opposite errors:
1. treating every project as if it needs an expensive research programme before work can begin; and
2. treating a persona, interview, click, social signal, AI summary, competitor list, or framework as proof of demand by itself.

Operating constraints
- One accountable operator, not a large company or research department.
- Some work is general software/product work; some is professional service and client website work; some is spatial/design work. Do not assume one industry.
- The operator works in Nigeria and may serve international clients. Country, income context, sector, language, data rules, and buyer behaviour are project-specific conditions, not universal instructions.
- Existing capabilities already cover Product Thinking, general Research Analysis, UI/UX, copywriting, conversion review, prospect research for a current specialised offer, and sales-enablement preparation.
- The output must preserve a strong model’s judgment. It should add evidence discipline, activation/exclusion boundaries, and useful stopping rules—not a compulsory discovery ritual or a fixed framework.
- Research and drafting are allowed conceptually. Outreach, recruitment, publishing, personal-data collection, CRM changes, advertising spend, purchases, and other external effects require separate explicit human approval and are out of scope.

Research questions
1. Separate these decisions clearly: product/problem discovery; market/segment choice; professional-service offer fit; message comprehension; willingness to pay; demand validation; competitor/alternative understanding; opportunity qualification; and post-launch learning. Which are genuinely related, and which should not be collapsed?
2. For each decision, compare what qualitative interviews, contextual observation, existing first-party data, desk research, competitor/alternative review, usability testing, smoke tests, concierge/manual tests, pre-sales or commitments, experiments, and simple operational observation can and cannot establish. Include common failure modes, sampling limits, recruitment bias, leading questions, social desirability, confounding, and false certainty.
3. Propose an evidence ladder that distinguishes hypothesis, anecdote, reported preference, observed behaviour, commitment, repeat behaviour, and causal evidence. It must state that stronger evidence is not always necessary: choose the cheapest credible evidence that could change the next decision.
4. What evidence is appropriate for low-traffic websites, early products, and professional services where conventional A/B testing is not credible? Identify useful small-study alternatives and their limits.
5. What does responsible willingness-to-pay or demand evidence look like for products and professional services? Distinguish interest, stated intent, commitments, paid work, pricing discovery, and actual repeat demand. Do not make universal pricing claims.
6. What should an AI assistant record about evidence provenance, date, source type, context, sample, geography, segment, contradiction, confidence, and unknowns? Which fields are essential versus needless bureaucracy for a small reversible task?
7. Define clean activation and exclusion boundaries between a possible Customer/Market Evidence capability and Product Thinking, Research Analysis, UI/UX research, Page CRO, Prospect Research, Sales Enablement, and any future Brand/Positioning work.
8. Decide whether this job requires: a new optional Growth skill; a shared reference/mode in existing skills; a project-specific procedure; or no new OS asset. Compare all four options using decision quality, operational burden, overlap, reversibility, and likely recurrence for a one-person studio.
9. Identify any 2024–2026 changes that materially affect AI-assisted customer or market research. Separate durable changes from tool/provider hype. Include risks of synthetic research, fabricated personas, data provenance failures, and false confidence.
10. Identify which familiar product-discovery, Lean Startup, Jobs-to-be-Done, market-research, and validation doctrines should remain conditional rather than becoming permanent universal OS rules.

Source requirements
- Prefer original research, systematic reviews/meta-analyses, official statistics or regulator guidance when relevant, primary platform documentation, and well-described empirical usability or experimentation research.
- Treat books, frameworks, accelerators, consultancies, agency articles, practitioner opinions, and AI summaries as secondary context only. Do not use them as the sole basis for a universal rule.
- Cite every material factual claim with a direct working link and publication or verification date.
- Clearly label sourced fact, inference, recommendation, and open uncertainty.
- Name country, industry, audience, channel, sample, and methodological limits that could change a conclusion.
- Do not give legal advice. Flag privacy, consent, research-participant, or data-protection questions that require project-specific review.

Required output
1. Executive synthesis: the smallest reliable approach to customer, market, and demand evidence for this Studio.
2. Decision-to-evidence matrix covering the distinct decisions above, including what each method can and cannot establish.
3. A proportionate evidence ladder and a "when no extra research is needed" rule.
4. Low-traffic/early-stage/professional-services alternatives to conventional A/B testing.
5. AI-era risks and durable safeguards.
6. Boundary map with existing Studio capabilities.
7. A four-option decision: new Growth capability, shared mode/reference, project procedure, or no asset—with recommendation, drawbacks, and conditions that would reverse it.
8. Candidate rules table: candidate rule, evidence/source, scope and limitation, proposed destination, and preserve/adapt/defer/reject decision.
9. Positive, negative-routing, and authority-stop scenarios that would test any future asset.
10. Disconfirmation: what evidence would prove your recommended capability shape wrong.

Return the complete report now. Do not ask for any additional attachment.
```

## Review gate after return

The returned report must be independently scored for source quality, claim discipline, context fit, evidence limits, ethical/authority fit, operational usefulness, and reversibility. It must not create or rewrite any skill by itself.
