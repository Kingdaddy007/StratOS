## Meaning of parity

Parity means every useful decision, safety boundary, or verification method has
a discoverable owner. It does not mean copying old workflow prose word for
word. These inherited behaviours were intentionally excluded: automatic memory
or state writes, mandatory all-resource loading, personal paths, unavailable
tools, automatic action chains, and unapproved external effects.
