## Source check and limits

The strongest source-backed conclusions are the safety and evidence-discipline boundaries:

- AI assistance does not excuse unsupported claims, synthetic proof, or false testimonials.
- Direct marketing, profiling, and data use are conditional on jurisdiction, channel, relationship, purpose, and current rules.
- AI-assisted web content still requires accuracy, quality, relevance, and genuine user value.
- Experiments, heuristics, heatmaps, interviews, metrics, and AI analyses each have limits; none is automatically proof.

The report's less-settled conclusions remain **design hypotheses**:

- seven active Growth entrypoints is a response to the brief's limit, not empirical proof that seven is the correct number;
- a separate `customer-and-market-evidence` skill may overlap Product Thinking and Research Analysis;
- a separate `growth-measurement-and-learning` skill may overlap Product Thinking, Testing, data/engineering, and `page-cro`;
- the cited landing-page optimisation paper is too narrow to act as the main source for general experimentation guidance;
- the narrative source is explicitly limited by access and must not justify a universal storytelling or persuasion rule.

## Approved operating direction

Adopt this provisional definition:

> Growth is optional decision support for fit, credibility, communication, access to appropriate opportunities, and observable learning around a product or service. It is not an autonomous campaign engine, persuasion machine, or substitute for product judgment, design, research, legal advice, or human approval.

Also adopt these durable boundaries:

1. Evidence must be labelled as fact, inference, recommendation, or unknown when it materially affects a decision or claim.
2. A claim, persona, score, funnel metric, heatmap, framework, or AI draft is not proof by itself.
3. Growth may prepare research, analysis, drafts, and proposed next actions; it cannot self-authorise outreach, publication, CRM changes, advertising spend, purchases, or personal-data collection.
4. Country, channel, sector, platform, privacy, and legal material belongs in conditional, verified references—not permanent universal instructions.
5. A short clear task must stay proportionate. An edit, rewrite, or low-risk page review does not need a mandatory evidence ledger, intake packet, or approval ceremony.

## Required preservation rules

- Do not delete, flatten, or mechanically shorten `brand-strategy` or `storytelling`. Their current spatial expertise remains protected until a later source-backed review gives every moved section a clear destination.
- Do not delete or generalise away `prospect-research`. Keep its current Cinematic Digital Showroom route usable; later add portability through optional profiles/references rather than removing the offer-specific mode.
- Do not treat the report’s "merge" recommendation as permission to make copywriting, copy editing, and storytelling one indistinguishable file. They may share a foundation while retaining distinct activation modes.
- Do not create an OS-wide personal-branding, social-media, SEO, ads, email, acquisition, retention, pricing, or growth-hacking skill merely because the category exists. Each needs a recurring Studio decision, a clean boundary, and evidence that a focused package adds value.
- Pricing/packaging and offer architecture are a possible missing job, especially for professional services, but remain a research question rather than an automatic eighth capability.

## Next research sequence

1. **Customer, market, and demand evidence** — first. Settle the evidence ladder, interviews, behavioural versus attitudinal evidence, sampling, willingness-to-pay, and the correct destination of this job.
2. **Portable brand, positioning, messaging, proof, and narrative** — second. Settle a shared portable core and identify exactly what stays in Brand Strategy and Storytelling's spatial references.
3. **Local conversion/measurement audit** — only when a real product or website, traffic, analytics, and privacy constraints are available.
4. **Opportunity/sales workflow audit** — only when a real client opportunity exposes a recurring limitation.

## Before any skill rewrite

For each proposed change, create a claim ledger naming the source, scope, limitation, destination, preserved material, and behaviour test. A change is rejected if it only makes a skill sound more modern, duplicates a stronger general capability, or turns a conditional practice into a universal command.
