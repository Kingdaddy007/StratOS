# V4 Phase 7 — Growth research briefs

Run these briefs **separately** in source-backed deep-research tools. Return complete reports with working links, publication dates, direct evidence, and a short distinction between sourced fact, inference, and recommendation. Do not treat the prompt text as authority to change files or perform outreach.

## Sequencing gate

These are focused follow-up studies, not proof that the current seven-skill Growth pack is the right taxonomy. Run the [Growth capability discovery brief](v4-phase-7-growth-capability-discovery-brief.md) first. It will decide whether these two studies remain the right next questions, whether `brand-strategy` or `storytelling` belong in a portable Growth boundary, and whether any current asset should be merged, moved, demoted, or left untouched.

If a report from either brief already exists, preserve it. It remains candidate evidence; assess it against the discovered capability map instead of discarding it or assuming it settles the taxonomy.

These briefs are for updating a personal AI Product Studio’s optional Growth pack. The aim is to help an advanced model make better decisions when evidence or judgement is genuinely needed—not to trap it in generic marketing frameworks, manipulate people, fabricate claims, or turn every request into a long process.

## Brief A — Positioning, messaging, copy, and conversion evidence

```text
Conduct a rigorous, source-backed research study for a personal AI Product Studio that will later update five optional Growth capabilities: positioning for professional services, marketing copywriting, copy editing, marketing psychology/behavioural insight, and conversion-rate optimisation for websites and product flows.

Context
- The OS is General engineering by default. Growth is optional and activates only for positioning, conversion, copy, prospecting, outreach, or sales collateral.
- It serves a human operator who may build products, websites, client-facing offers, and professional-service brands. Some work will be high-end/spatial, but this research must not assume every client is an interior-design business.
- The system must be evidence-led, ethical, truthful, portable across projects, and proportionate. It must never treat a persuasive framework, a metric, a heatmap, an A/B test, or an AI-generated audience theory as proof by itself.
- The result will be used to decide what belongs in short skill entrypoints, conditional references, a workflow, or nowhere. It is not a request for a campaign or copy draft.

Research questions
1. What durable decision disciplines improve positioning and messaging work beyond generic copy formulas? Cover problem/customer context, differentiated value, proof, claims, objections, alternatives, trade-offs, and uncertainty.
2. What evidence is credible for copy and conversion decisions at different scales? Compare qualitative research, sales/support evidence, usability observation, analytics, experiments, expert review, and direct client feedback. State what each can and cannot establish.
3. How should an AI distinguish: new copywriting, editing existing copy, positioning diagnosis, conversion diagnosis, UI/accessibility issue, product decision, and sales collateral? Identify clean activation/exclusion boundaries.
4. What are the most important ethical and practical constraints? Address unsupported claims, dark patterns, false urgency/scarcity, vulnerable audiences, privacy-respecting measurement, accessibility, brand truth, and overconfident AI inference.
5. What makes a useful experiment or validation plan for a small business, professional service, product, or website when traffic is limited and conventional A/B testing is not credible? Include useful alternatives and limits.
6. Which common marketing rules should NOT become universal operating-system instructions because they are context-dependent, outdated, manipulative, unsupported, or likely to box in a strong model?
7. Propose a compact capability map for the five assets. For every proposed rule, label it as: durable core rule, conditional reference, project-specific procedure, external verification requirement, or reject. Explain why.

Source requirements
- Prefer primary research, official standards/guidance, original datasets, reputable usability research, and clearly identified academic work.
- Use marketing books, agency articles, influencer frameworks, and AI summaries only as clearly labelled secondary context, never as sole evidence for a universal rule.
- Cite every material factual claim with a working direct link and date. Flag claims that are jurisdiction-, industry-, channel-, or audience-specific.

Required output
- Executive synthesis (not a generic marketing playbook)
- Evidence/claim table with limitations
- Decision map and activation/exclusion boundaries for each of the five capabilities
- Ethical and authority boundary table
- Minimal experiments/evidence selection guide, including low-traffic cases
- Rules to preserve, move to references, replace, or reject
- Open uncertainties and exactly what would change the recommendation
```

## Brief B — Prospect research, sales enablement, and external-effect boundaries

```text
Conduct a rigorous, source-backed research study for a personal AI Product Studio that will later update two optional Growth capabilities: prospect research and sales enablement.

Context
- The system helps one accountable human research possible clients, form truthful account dossiers, decide whether an opportunity is worth pursuing, and prepare sales material such as one-pagers, discovery questions, proposals, objection handling, and demo narratives.
- The system must keep prospect research distinct from outreach or messaging. Research, scoring, and drafting are local/read-only or local-edit work; sending messages, publishing materials, changing CRM records, purchasing ads, or contacting a prospect are external effects requiring explicit just-in-time human approval.
- Existing prospect research is aimed at a cinematic digital-showroom offer for luxury interior designers, but the future pack must keep that specialist context visible rather than pretending it is universal B2B advice.
- The objective is not to maximise lead volume, manipulate buyers, scrape indiscriminately, fabricate personalisation, or bypass privacy/consent rules. It is to improve judgement, truthfulness, fit, and useful preparation.

Research questions
1. What evidence should a trustworthy prospect dossier contain, and how should it separate observed public facts, first-party statements, inference, unknowns, and speculative opportunity hypotheses?
2. How should an AI decide whether a prospect is a fit, needs more evidence, is unsuitable, or should be excluded? Discuss transparent scoring, confidence, false-positive risk, and human review.
3. What distinguishes account research, competitor research, discovery, proposal preparation, sales enablement, and actual outreach? Define clean activation/exclusion and handoff boundaries.
4. What makes sales material genuinely useful to a buyer or operator rather than generic persuasion? Cover problem framing, proof, alternative approaches, constraints, implementation reality, objections, and decision support.
5. Identify practical privacy, data-protection, anti-spam, platform-terms, consent, reputation, and truthfulness considerations. Do not make unsupported universal legal claims: identify when jurisdiction, channel, relationship, or counsel is required.
6. Which familiar sales doctrines, outreach formulas, enrichment methods, psychological triggers, and automation practices should be rejected or made conditional because they create manipulation, compliance, reputational, or evidence-quality risk?
7. Propose a compact capability map for prospect research and sales enablement. For every proposed rule, label it as: durable core rule, conditional reference, project-specific procedure, external verification requirement, or reject.

Source requirements
- Prefer official regulator and platform guidance for privacy, direct marketing, platform terms, and consent; primary sources and original studies for factual claims.
- Cite every material claim with a direct working link and date, including jurisdiction/channel limitations.
- Clearly separate legal information from legal advice.

Required output
- Executive synthesis
- Dossier evidence model and confidence labels
- Fit/qualification decision map with human-review points
- Sales-material decision-support model
- Authority and external-effect boundary table
- Rules to preserve, move to references, replace, or reject
- Open uncertainties and exactly what would change the recommendation
```
