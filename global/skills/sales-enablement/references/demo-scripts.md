# Decision-Fit Call and Demo Plans

Load this reference when preparing a conversation. Use a sequence that fits the buyer's current decision. Do not force discovery, demonstration, or a close when a different step is more useful.

## Prepare

Record the call purpose, participants, what is known, what is inferred, permitted materials, decision authority, and desired next action. Mark customer data, unreleased capability, security details, and commercial terms that require explicit handling.

## Select a Sequence

| Situation | Useful sequence | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early uncertainty | Confirm purpose → ask questions → summarise attributed facts → agree next step | Presenting an assumed pain as fact. |
| Buyer needs a capability review | Confirm evaluation criteria → show a scoped workflow → state limits → collect questions → agree evidence/next step | Showing unavailable, mock, or staged behaviour as standard product behaviour. |
| Technical or procurement review | Confirm requirements → answer from traceable sources → mark unknowns → record follow-ups | Guessing certification, security, availability, or contract status. |
| Commercial decision | Confirm scope/options → surface assumptions and value scenario → identify authority → agree decision path | Treating an estimate, discount, or timeline as approved terms. |

## Demonstrate Safely

- Show only current, permitted capability. Label mock data, prototype paths, beta behaviour, and future work.
- Map a demonstrated workflow to a buyer-stated need or state that it is an illustrative example.
- Keep proof attributable. Do not improvise metrics, customer stories, or implementation promises.
- Pause for questions at decision points. Treat stated objections and priorities as current evidence; treat silence and role stereotypes as hypotheses.

## Close Responsibly

End with a factual summary: agreed facts, assumptions, unanswered questions, follow-up owner, and next action. Do not manufacture urgency or commit price, scope, terms, guarantee, or timeline without approval.
