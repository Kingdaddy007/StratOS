## Source Snapshot

Use jurisdiction-specific sources only after the relevant market, audience, channel, and facts are known. This package does not provide legal advice.

- [FTC Advertising FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business): claim substantiation, express and implied claims, material omissions, and contextual interpretation. Verified 2026-08-19.
- [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking): honest endorsements and material connections. Verified 2026-08-19.
- [FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers): deceptive-review and testimonial practices. Verified 2026-08-19.
- [ICO PECR electronic-mail marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules/): UK electronic-marketing requirements depend on recipient and context. Verified 2026-08-19.
