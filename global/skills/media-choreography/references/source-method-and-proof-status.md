# Source Method and Proof Status

Load when media may be captured, licensed, rendered, composited, generated, or reused and the choice affects truth, quality, cost, rights, geometry, or continuity.

## Separate what the asset is from what it implies

| Status | May support | Do not imply without further evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Verified client work** | Documented project, credited role, visible decisions | Outcomes or authorship beyond the record |
| **Client-reported material** | Attributed reported facts / experience | Independent verification |
| **Licensed / commissioned media** | Licensed context, atmosphere, product, or place | That the client designed the depicted space |
| **New capture / film of actual work** | New documented view of the real subject | Facts outside what is actually captured / known |
| **Render of actual / proposed work** | Controlled representation with traceable project identity | Unobserved detail as verified built fact |
| **Composite / layered plates** | Controlled separation / transition | Seamless truth if geometry, contact, or source identity is synthetic |
| **Generated exploration** | Mood, study, possible composition / transition | Completed work, real people, real place, real result |
| **Generated client-facing art direction** | Explicitly illustrative / synthetic visual role | Project proof unless separately verified |
| **Unknown** | Question to resolve | Publication-ready proof |

A beautiful image may still be unsuitable evidence.

## Choose the method for the requirement

| Method | Strong when | Limits to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Existing stills** | Real work and decisive details already exist | Resolution, crop, absent viewpoints, project identity |
| **New photography / film** | Actual subject can be captured with chosen view / light | Access, rights, budget, schedule, consistency |
| **3D / architectural render** | Viewpoint, geometry, or material states need control | Accuracy, production cost, credibility |
| **Composite / layered plates** | Specific separation or controlled transition is needed | Edges, contact, perspective, light, newly exposed pixels |
| **Generated media** | Exploration or explicitly synthetic visual direction benefits from speed | Geometry drift, factual ambiguity, continuity, rights, final quality |
| **Licensed / archival** | Authentic context, history, product, place, or atmosphere matters | Provenance, usage terms, misleading association |

Use combinations only when each source has a clear role.

Do not choose generation merely because real assets are inconvenient. First ask whether the concept should adapt to what is true.

## Test source feasibility

Ask what information the intended crop, camera move, parallax, transformation, or transition reveals that the source does not contain.

Examples:

- crop travel requires overscan, not unseen geometry;
- true camera travel requires related views, film, a model, render, or synthetic reconstruction;
- object separation requires silhouette, contact shadow, reflection, and background support;
- seamless project transition requires identity clarity, not merely matching colour.

For a decisive asset inspect:

- native dimensions;
- orientation;
- focal subject;
- required crop / overscan;
- material detail;
- compression tolerance;
- mobile crop;
- rights / attribution;
- project identity.

State when more capture, related views, geometry, or a simpler visual operation is required.

## Rights and approval

Record when relevant:

- who supplied the source;
- what use is permitted;
- required credit;
- whether alteration is permitted;
- whether public use is approved;
- whether the media depicts real or proposed work.

Absent rights or role evidence remains unknown.

Do not claim a client approved generated or altered media merely because the asset exists.
