# Audio and Dialogue Direction

## Specify the Audio Job

State whether the clip needs silence, natural ambience, sound effects, music character, spoken dialogue, supplied audio influence, or a reference voice. Describe the desired timing relationship between sound and image, then verify the selected provider's visible audio/reference controls before writing provider syntax.

For dialogue, define speaker, exact words where appropriate, framing, competing action, intended language, and the review criterion. Keep spoken content proportionate to clip duration. Treat lip-sync, pronunciation, speaker separation, and audio continuity as outcomes to inspect, not promised capabilities.

## Review and Diagnose

Check intelligibility, speaker attribution, timing, unwanted music or ambience, distortion, audio drift, and whether the output changed the intended message. Preserve the input, settings, result, and observed issue. Change one plausible cause at a time; do not try to coerce a safety system or imply that an uploaded audio file will be played unchanged without verified support.
