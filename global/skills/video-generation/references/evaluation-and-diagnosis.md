# Evaluation and Diagnosis

## Preserve Evidence

Record provider, surface, exact model/API ID, date, access state, prompt, reference roles, settings, visible limits, output, failure/refusal text, and credit/billing state. A successful rerun is not a root cause by itself.

## Diagnose in Order

1. Access, region, plan, credits, activation, or unavailable control.
2. Unsupported input, task mode, surface syntax, or locked parameter.
3. Rights, likeness, confidentiality, safety, or policy boundary.
4. Conflicting or insufficient references.
5. Ambiguous brief, excess beat density, or unstable composition.
6. Ordinary model variance.

Write one hypothesis, change one variable, define the expected observation, and compare the result. Stop for current provider guidance when the evidence cannot distinguish a product limitation from a transient failure.

Never translate, disguise, retry, or use an alternative provider as a way to bypass a safeguard. A provider switch is an explicit new route with its own active-surface and rights checks.
