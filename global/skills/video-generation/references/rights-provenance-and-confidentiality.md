# Rights, Provenance, and Confidentiality

Inventory every supplied image, video, voice, music track, logo, artwork, location, person, and brand. Record ownership, licence or consent, intended territory/channel, and whether upload or publication is approved.

Separate three questions: whether the asset may be used, whether the selected platform permits it, and whether the planned client delivery is approved. A platform response does not grant rights; a client asset does not establish confidential processing; a visually successful output is not legal clearance.

Before upload or delivery, verify the active provider's treatment of real people, voices, minors, brands, copyrighted material, data retention/review, visible watermarking, invisible watermarking, and provenance credentials. Disclose material provenance restrictions to the client. Never remove, hide, or advise circumventing a safety or provenance control.

Stop for explicit approval before uploading third-party or sensitive material, generating paid media, or publishing/delivering an asset.
