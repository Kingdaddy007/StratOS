# Growth Direct Selection

Load this profile-scoped procedure only after the Growth pack is selected. Select the smallest owning capability; do not turn a direct task into a workflow because it contains commercial language.

| Request | Direct owner | Add another route only when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New or materially changed paid offer, major proposal, scope, or price hypothesis | `offer-architecture` | The decision needs versioned evidence, specialist challenge, human approval, and archive: use `commercial-decision-record`. |
| New copy or material rewrite from an understood offer | `copywriting` | Audience, offer, proof, or positioning remains materially uncertain. |
| Tighten or review approved copy | `copy-editing` | The edit would change the audience, offer, promise, or factual claim. |
| Positioning, professional-service posture, or differentiated promise | `expert-positioning` | It changes the commercial object or needs a human-reviewed offer. |
| Page, signup, inquiry, or flow friction | `page-cro` with `ui-ux` as needed | A live, reversible intervention needs a learning record. |
| Public opportunity or account preparation | `prospect-research` | Buyer-facing collateral needs `sales-enablement`; contact always needs separate approval. |
| Proposal deck, one-pager, objection aid, or demo narrative | `sales-enablement` | Offer, feasibility, proof, or claim boundaries are unresolved. |
| Customer, market, demand, or alternative uncertainty | `product-thinking` or `research-analysis` | A material offer decision needs the evidence in an offer record. |

Never send, publish, advertise, price, negotiate, collect data, mutate a CRM, accept a contract, or take payment from this procedure. Those remain separate explicit human decisions.
