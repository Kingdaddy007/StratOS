# Customer, Market, and Demand Evidence

**Type:** conditional shared reference  
**Owner:** Product & Strategy  
**Status:** verified research synthesis, 2026-08-19

## Purpose

Use this reference only when the next product, service, offer, positioning, conversion, prospect, or launch decision depends on an unresolved claim about customers, markets, adoption, willingness to pay, or demand.

It supplies decision-to-evidence matching, provenance, and honest limits. It does **not** create a compulsory discovery phase, a new Growth skill, permission to make external claims, or authority to collect data or contact people.

## Do not load for

- A clear, low-risk, reversible implementation or ordinary copy edit where the action itself is informative.
- Purely technical, security, architecture, or database work with no customer/market decision.
- A request to summarise known sources, inspect competitors, improve an interface, or draft approved copy when no demand claim is being made.

Use Product Thinking for the product decision, Research Analysis for general source research, UI/UX for task and interface quality, Page CRO for page/flow friction, Prospect Research for the current Cinematic Digital Showroom target list, and Sales Enablement for approved buyer-facing preparation. This reference contributes only the evidence discipline when their decision concerns people, market, or demand uncertainty.

## Proportionate route

1. **Name the decision and dominant uncertainty.** What would change the next action: problem reality, segment, offer fit, comprehension, price, demand, or post-launch behaviour?
2. **Use existing evidence first.** Prefer relevant first-party records, direct observation, verified public material, and known constraints before proposing new research.
3. **Choose the cheapest credible evidence that could change the decision.** Do not select a method because a framework requires it.
4. **State the evidence status and limits.** A conclusion must name what it supports, what it does not support, contradictions, and material unknowns.
5. **Propose—not authorise—the next action.** Keep host and user approval gates intact.

No additional customer/market work is needed when the action is reversible, the cost of being wrong is low, relevant direct evidence already exists, no material public/privacy/safety exposure is created, and the action itself will produce useful observation.

## Evidence status is decision-relative

Use these labels instead of a universal "validated" stamp:

| Status | Examples | It may support | It does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hypothesis | Assumption, concept, untested positioning idea | A question or smallest credible test | That a problem or market exists |
| Reported | Interview statement, survey intent, self-report | Vocabulary, context, objection, candidate hypothesis | Prevalence, payment, repeat demand, causal effect |
| Observed | Real workflow, prototype task, support record, usage event | A bounded behaviour or friction in its context | Motive, prevalence, future payment, broad demand |
| Qualified commitment | Defined buyer accepts a bounded next step, grants access, or accepts a proposal | Near-term fit, authority, urgency, scope for that buyer | Broad demand or repeat economics |
| Paid / repeat behaviour | Paid pilot, purchase, renewal, repeat use, referral | Commercial or sustained fit in the tested context | Market size or universal causality |
| Causal evidence | Credible comparison with adequate allocation, measurement, and uncertainty | Estimated effect in the tested population and period | Universal result across audiences, channels, or time |

## Select methods by the question

| Decision | Credible starting evidence | Do not overclaim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem/workflow discovery | Recent behaviour, workarounds, observation, targeted conversations | A real problem does not by itself prove urgency, reachability, or payment |
| Segment or market choice | Compatible official/context data, alternatives, access/buyer evidence | A demographic persona or large category is not automatically attractive or reachable |
| Message comprehension | Task-based comprehension or usability observation with relevant people | Comprehension is not demand, trust, payment, or conversion lift |
| Offer fit / willingness to pay | Concrete scope, qualified buyer context, real proposal/pilot/commitment where approved | Stated interest or a survey price is not payment |
| Low-traffic conversion question | Existing enquiries/support/search evidence, task sessions, one reversible change with an observation window | A heuristic, heatmap, or sparse before/after period does not establish causality |
| Post-launch learning | Instrumented behaviour, support patterns, operational observation, cohort/time context | Correlation can be confounded by channel mix, novelty, seasonality, or implementation changes |

There is no magic interview count or fixed usability-participant number. State the participant or source definition, selection, context, variation, contradictions, and the decision that the evidence may influence.

## Lightweight evidence record

For a material decision, record only the fields that prevent evidence laundering:

```text
Decision / dominant uncertainty:
Evidence: source or observation, date, context/segment, reported or observed status
What it supports / what it does not support:
Contradictions and unknowns:
Confidence and proposed next action:
```

Add sample/coverage, geography, participant role, consent/data-use status, AI transformation record, or quantitative uncertainty only when their absence could change a consequential, public, sensitive, costly, or difficult-to-reverse decision.

## AI and authority boundaries

- AI may help retrieve, structure, compare, code, and find contradictions. AI-generated personas, simulated interviews, and fluent summaries are hypotheses or working aids—not customer evidence, quotations, commitments, or demand validation.
- Read-only public desk research within the task scope does not require a new approval gate.
- Stop for explicit human approval before recruiting or inviting participants, contacting prospects, collecting personal/sensitive data, publishing a smoke test, spending on advertising, accepting payment, making a public market/performance/customer claim, updating a CRM, or creating another external effect.
- Never invent a testimonial, review, customer quote, purchase signal, market statistic, consent basis, or proof. Flag jurisdiction, channel, relationship, and data-use questions rather than giving blanket legal conclusions.

## Verification cues

- A low-risk copy improvement remains direct work; this reference is not loaded merely because the page is commercial.
- A comprehension study cannot be reported as demand validation.
- A prospect dossier can use public evidence and confidence labels, but cannot send outreach or infer private intent.
- A low-traffic page review may recommend a reversible change and observation plan, but cannot promise conversion lift.
