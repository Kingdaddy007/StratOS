## Required Active-Surface Check

Record the product URL or endpoint, country/region, exact model label/API ID, task mode, available input roles, file/count/duration controls, aspect ratio/resolution, credits/activation, safety/likeness controls, export/provenance, and applicable terms.

Do not treat `Lite`, `Mini`, `Fast`, or `Pro` as portable categories. A suffix is part of an exact label, not evidence of a particular speed, quality, price, or capability.
